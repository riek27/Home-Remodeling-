Our Story

Guardians of the Sudd Wetlands

Sudd Environment Agency was born out of a deep concern for South Sudan's unique ecosystems and the communities that depend on them. We focus on practical solutions that blend environmental protection with community empowerment, ensuring that both nature and people thrive together.

Mission & Vision
Our Compass

Mission & Vision

Mission: To promote environmental sustainability through climate change education, pollution reduction, WASH improvements, and protection of the Sudd wetlands and wildlife.

Vision: A resilient South Sudan where healthy ecosystems support prosperous communities and iconic species are protected for future generations.

Our Projects

Every project is designed to combine environmental stewardship with community empowerment, focusing on sustainable food systems, climate resilience, and inclusive development.

Ulang County, Upper Nile

Demonstration Farm

A sign of hope in Ulang County. Our demonstration farm is turning green, showcasing climate‑smart agriculture techniques that increase food production while protecting the environment. The farm serves as a learning hub for local farmers, promoting sustainable practices and resilience against climate shocks.

Food Security Climate Adaptation
Ulang County, Upper Nile

Skills for Food Security

We stand with communities to equip them with practical skills that improve food security. Through hands‑on training in modern farming, post‑harvest handling, and nutrition, we empower families to grow their own food sustainably. The initiative is already showing good progress in Ulang County.

Livelihoods Nutrition
Ulang County, Upper Nile

Inclusive Climate‑Smart Farming

Bringing smiles to the faces of people with disabilities through climate‑smart agriculture. This project supports resilience by providing accessible farming techniques and tools, enabling everyone to participate in food production and adapt to climate challenges.
